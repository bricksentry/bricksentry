# Code of Conduct

bricksentry is a small open-source project, and everyone who uses it, reports a bug or sends a change is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Most disagreements are about code, not people.
- Be specific and constructive in reviews and issues. Explain the problem and, where you can, suggest a fix.
- Help newcomers. Everyone asked a first question once.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring a maintainer's request to stop.

## Where this applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you represent the project, such as its LinkedIn page or community events.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/Brahmawritings) or the project's [LinkedIn page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bricksentry-org/). Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer will review every report and may respond with a reminder, a warning, removal of comments, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces, depending on what happened.

For security problems, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
